# Genesis Capital Workspace

<!-- nav:top -->
> **[Docs roadmap](../README.md)** › **Genesis Capital workspace** — Genesis Capital staff, implementers, presenters
<!-- /nav:top -->

Genesis Capital is the demonstration workspace the seed builds: a (fictional) Mauritian financial-services group
with six business units — lending, wealth, funds, securities, corporate advisory and forex — configured entirely on
the Genesis platform as Builder metadata (19 modules, 91 objects, 89 workflows, 15 dashboards, a client and
introducer portal, English and French).
