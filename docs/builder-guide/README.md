# Builder Guide

<!-- nav:top -->
> **[Docs roadmap](../README.md)** › **Builder guide** — CRM administrators configuring the workspace
<!-- /nav:top -->

Screen-by-screen instructions for administrators configuring a workspace in the Builder (`/builder`, visible to the
Admin and Super Admin roles). The guides are numbered in the order a workspace is built — each assumes the ones
before it:

| Stage | Guides | You end up with |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Blueprint** | 01 Modules & objects → 02 Form builder → 03 Pipeline builder → 04 Wizards → 05 Dashboard builder | The data model and the screens |
| **2. People & access** | 06 Permissions → 07 Roles & levels → 08 Employees → 09 Org chart → 10 Business units & barriers | Who works here and what each person sees |
| **3. Automation** | 11 Assignment rules → 12 Workflows → 13 Lead scoring → 14 SLA engine | What the CRM does by itself |
| **4. Channels** | 15 Email templates → 16 Push notifications → 17 Integrations → 18 Integration health → 19 Social media | How it talks to people and systems |
| **5. Look** | 20 Appearance & branding | Your colours, logo and fonts |

> **Before you start**, read [Understanding Genesis](../understanding/README.md) steps 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 — they explain
> the ideas these screens configure. The reasoning behind each area is in [Administration](../administration/README.md).
